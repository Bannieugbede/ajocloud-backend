# Open flexible Ajo contribution questions

ADR-002 authorizes whole-number contribution units and no platform float only. The following remain unresolved:

1. May more than one payout unit be paid in a contribution period, and how are units grouped into periods?
2. What collection threshold makes a payout eligible when a contribution is late?
3. Is a shortfall held, rescheduled, covered by a member reserve, or handled through removal/replacement?
4. Can unit quantities change before lock, and what approvals are required?
5. What minimum/maximum unit quantity and total obligation apply per member and KYC tier?
6. Can a payout entitlement be transferred, and what contractual/KYC checks apply?
7. Are fees inside or outside the reconciled contribution pool?
8. Are proportional, fractional, or independent contribution plans ever permitted? Current answer: no.
9. May platform or third-party capital fund a shortfall? Current answer: no.

No unresolved option may be inferred from product examples or provider behaviour.
