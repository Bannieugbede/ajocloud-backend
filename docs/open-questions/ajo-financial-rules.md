# Open Ajo financial questions

No item below is authorized by the current implementation.

1. What happens when one or more scheduled contributions are late at payout time: delay all payout, partial payout, member reserve, or removal?
2. Approved baseline: both affected owners approve within 72 hours; administrators may initiate/reject for policy violations. Product must still decide whether every member-initiated swap needs a separate administrator veto/approval.
3. Can groups open with fewer slots than configured capacity, and when is final capacity frozen?
4. Are multiple payout recipients per period ever allowed? If so, define exact allocation and solvency evidence.
5. Are group-admin penalties capped, reviewed, and represented as revenue, member liability, or contribution adjustments?
6. Are contribution/payout fees inside or outside the advertised pool? Define ledger accounts and disclosure.
7. Can a member transfer ownership of a slot, and what KYC/contract changes are required?
8. ADR-002 permits different amounts only as whole-number quantities of a configured smallest unit. Arbitrary custom amounts and fractional units remain prohibited.
9. May platform capital ever cover deficits? Current answer is no; approval would require legal/compliance review and a new ADR.
10. What cancellation/refund rules apply before and after lock?
