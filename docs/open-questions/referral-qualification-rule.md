# Open referral qualification rule

The phrase “Ajo or Food Ajo #5” is ambiguous and is not an approved reward rule. Product and compliance must clarify whether it means a fifth contribution, five qualifying activities, a numbered package, an amount, or something else.

Until clarified, campaigns must be effective-dated data defining qualifying product/event, settled transaction count, minimum amount, KYC tier, reward amount/currency, caps, expiry, and fraud restrictions. Registration alone never qualifies. Failed or reversed transactions never release a reward, and a reversed qualifying transaction must cancel or reverse any resulting reward through the ledger.
